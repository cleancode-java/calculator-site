How these numbers are calculated

This calculator estimates your UK self-employed taxes using official HMRC tax bands and rates.

It includes:

  • Income Tax (progressive / tiered bands)
  • Personal Allowance, including reduction for income above £100,000
  • Class 4 National Insurance
  • Profit calculation based on allowable expenses

Effective marginal rate

The effective marginal rate shows how much tax applies to your next £1 of income, not to your total profit.

Between £100,000 and £125,140, the marginal rate is higher because:

  • your income is taxed at the 40% higher rate, and
  • part of your Personal Allowance is withdrawn, making additional income taxable.

This results in a higher effective marginal tax rate in this income range, due to the combined effect of higher-rate Income Tax and the withdrawal of the Personal Allowance.

National Insurance is calculated separately and is not affected by Personal Allowance.

This calculator applies all taxes progressively and does not apply a single flat rate to your income.

Income Tax is calculated automatically using UK progressive tax bands.
Class 4 National Insurance is calculated automatically using UK profit thresholds (6% and 2%).

National Insurance (Class 4) is calculated progressively. Different rates apply to different portions of your profit: 0% up to the lower threshold, 6% on the middle range, and 2% above the upper limit.

How to read this table

This table provides a detailed breakdown of your annual profit, showing the amounts taxed at each rate for Income Tax and Class 4 National Insurance.

  • Category: The type of income, tax, or contribution.
  • Rate: The percentage rate applied to the taxable amount.
  • Band range (£): The income range to which this rate applies.
  • Taxable amount (£): The portion of your profit within this band.
  • Tax due (£): The amount of tax or National Insurance calculated for this band.

Key points:

  • Income Tax is calculated progressively across bands: Personal Allowance (0%), Basic Rate (20%), Higher Rate (40%), Additional Rate (45%).
  • Class 4 National Insurance is calculated progressively: 0%, 6%, 2% on applicable profit ranges.
  • Net Income represents your take-home profit after all taxes and contributions.

How to print or save your table?

For your convenience, you can print the table results or save them as a PDF on your computer.

Enter a title for your table in the field under Report, for example: "Tax and Income Report for John Smith: 2024-2025". Then click the Export to PDF button.

Your print dialog will open, where you can adjust settings as needed, such as:

  • Printer (select your physical printer for paper, or a PDF printer/converter to save as a file)
  • Number of copies (e.g., 1)
  • Layout (Portrait)
  • Pages: choose only page 1, as the table is on the first page and the remaining pages are blank. (we're working on expanding the print output to include charts soon.)
